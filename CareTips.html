Care Tips for Your Dog
Category Care Tip Details
Nutrition Provide fresh water and balanced, age-appropriate dog food. Daily; avoid foods toxic to dogs like chocolate, grapes, and onions.
Exercise Take your dog for daily walks and play interactive games. Train the dog regularly. 1-2 times daily, depending on breed, age, and energy level.
Grooming Brush their coat, trim nails, and clean their ears. Brush weekly; trim nails and check ears monthly or as needed.
Dental Health Brush their teeth with dog-safe toothpaste or offer dental chews. 2-3 times per week minimum; daily is ideal.
Veterinary Care Schedule routine check-ups, vaccinations to protect from rabies, and parasite prevention. Annual wellness exams; monthly flea, tick, and heartworm prevention.
Remember to consult with your veterinarian for personalized care advice. Learn more from Humane World for Animals.
